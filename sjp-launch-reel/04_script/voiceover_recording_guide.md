# Recording the voiceover yourself (phone)

Record in a quiet room with soft furnishings, phone 15 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side. One file per line, named `01.wav` … `18.wav`. Leave half a second of silence at both ends. Aim for the duration shown; the edit re-times itself if you differ.

|---|---|---|---|
