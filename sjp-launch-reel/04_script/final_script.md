## Fact notes
- **Nalanda** was in ruins by about 1200 CE. Who destroyed it, and how, is debated by historians, so the script names no one.
- **1835:** Governor-General Bentinck's resolution of 7 March 1835 directed East India Company education funds to English education. Existing Sanskrit and Arabic colleges were not closed, but new stipends and the printing of oriental works were stopped.
- The viral 'Macaulay quote' ("I have travelled across the length and breadth of India…") is fabricated. Do not use it.
- The Hampi (1868), Martand (1873) and Nalanda (1872) images are real archival photographs of the ruins as they stood then. They are not evidence of who damaged them or when.
- La Martinière (Lucknow, photographed 1858) and the c. 1870 student portrait illustrate the colonial-era education setting. They are not images of the 1835 Act itself.
