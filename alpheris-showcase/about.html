About Us

Akshat Singh

A Master’s student in International Management at Hochschule Fresenius in Berlin, with hands-on experience in building and managing social media brands, I am highly motivated to contribute my skills, creativity, and passion to your team.

I have always been a highly practical and action-oriented individual with a strong passion for creating meaningful work. But to understand the true impact of what I create, I inevitably have to depend on how people respond to it because that is the only real measure of whether my work resonates. When people appreciate it, I feel a deep sense of fulfilment. This realisation naturally drew me toward marketing, a field that allows me to develop ideas, present them to the world, and learn from real audience feedback. This continuous cycle of creation, reflection, and improvement drives me to refine my skills and consistently innovate.
