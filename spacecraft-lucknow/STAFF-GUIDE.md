# Space Craft website — staff guide

Products daalne aur orders sambhalne ka tareeka. Website kharab hone ka darr mat rakhiye: sab kuch dobara edit ho sakta hai.

## Login

**Pehli baar:** aapko ek link milega. Use kholke apna password set kijiye (kam se kam 10 akshar). Link 24 ghante chalta hai.
Password bhool gaye? Login page par **Forgot password?** dabaiye.

1. Browser mein kholiye: **`<website>/admin`**
2. Apna email aur password daaliye. (Har staff ka alag login hota hai — password kisi se share mat kijiye.)
3. Kaam khatam ho toh **Log out** dabaiye, khaas kar shop ke shared computer par.

## Naya product daalna

Ek product = ek design / item (jaise "Fluted Louver — Oak Tone").
Uske **options** = alag size, colour ya finish, har ek ka apna price aur stock.

1. Left menu → **Products & stock** → **+ New product**
2. Bhariye:
   - **Product name** — jo customer ko dikhega
   - **Category** — zaroori (Louvers, Laminates…)
   - **Brand** — agar hai
   - **Description** — 2–4 line, simple bhasha mein
   - **GST rate (%)** aur **HSN code** — supplier ke bill par likhe hote hain. Ye GST invoice par chhapte hain, isliye sahi bhariye.
     Jo price aap daalenge, woh **GST ke saath** wala price hai.
   - **Specifications** — har line mein `Naam: value`, jaise `Thickness: 8 mm`.
     Sirf wahi likhiye jo pakka pata ho (brand ke catalogue / box se).
3. **Create product** dabaiye.
4. Ab **Photos** → **Upload photos**. Pehli photo "Cover" banti hai (list mein wahi dikhti hai).
   - Saaf roshni mein, seedha khinchi photo. JPG/PNG, 5 MB se chhoti.
   - Kisi aur ki website se photo copy mat kijiye.
5. Neeche **Add an option** mein har size/colour ke liye:
   - **Option name** — jaise `8 ft × 4 ft, Matt`
   - **SKU** — aapka apna code, har option ka alag (jaise `LV-OAK-8X4`)
   - **Price (₹)** — khaali chhodenge toh "Price on request" dikhega, online nahi bikega
   - **Sale price** — sirf offer ho tab, normal price se kam
   - **Stock** — kitne piece abhi shop mein hain
   - **Add option** dabaiye
6. **View on site ↗** dabake check kar lijiye.

**Product chhupana ho** (delete nahi karna): "Show on website" ka tick hata ke Save.
**Homepage par dikhana ho**: "Feature on homepage" tick.

## Stock update karna

Products & stock → product kholiye → option mein **Stock** number badaliye →
"Reason for stock change" mein likhiye (jaise "naya maal aaya") → **Save option**.

- Order **paid** hote hi stock apne aap kam ho jaata hai.
- Paid order **cancel** karne par stock apne aap wapas aa jaata hai.
- Product page ke neeche "Recent stock changes" mein har badlav dikhta hai.
- Dashboard par "Running low" mein 5 ya kam stock wale items dikhte hain.

## Orders

Naya paid order aane par email aata hai (agar alert set hai). Phir:

1. Left menu → **Orders** → **New — paid** tab
2. Order kholiye → items, customer ka phone aur address dikhega
3. Customer ko call ya **Message on WhatsApp** karke delivery time fix kijiye. **Porter ka delivery charge** bhi customer ko pehle bata dijiye — ye online payment mein shaamil nahi hai.
   Order page par **Invoice ↗** se GST invoice khul jaata hai (Print / Save as PDF).
4. **Change status** se aage badhaiye:
   **Paid → Being prepared → Out for delivery → Delivered**
   "Note" mein likha message customer ko tracking page par dikhta hai (jaise "Saturday subah delivery").
5. Order cancel karna ho → status **Cancelled**. Dhyaan rakhiye:
   - Paisa **apne aap wapas nahi jaata** — refund Razorpay Dashboard se karna hota hai (payment ID order page par likha hai).
   - Cancel ke baad order dobara badla nahi ja sakta.

"Unpaid / abandoned" tab mein woh orders hain jinka payment poora nahi hua — inka maal mat bhejiye.

Customer apna order **`<website>/track`** par order number + mobile number se dekh sakta hai.

## Mat kijiye

- Price ya stock andaaze se mat daaliye — galat price par bhi customer pay kar sakta hai.
- Brand ke naam ya specification mein jhooti / bina confirm baat mat likhiye.
- Category tab tak delete nahi hoti jab tak usme product hain — pehle products move kijiye.
- Paise wale order ka maal bhejne se pehle check kijiye ki status **Paid** hai.

Kuch samajh na aaye ya galti ho jaaye — ghabraiye mat, rukiye aur poochhiye.
